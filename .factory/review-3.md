## Demo and sandbox verification

- One click on **Try it with sample data** opened `/?demo=1` without setup or an account.
- The first 390 × 844 demo screen showed the persistent banner, Reset and Exit actions, and a complete realistic sample record. The record occupied y=518.45 through y=818.06, inside the viewport.
- The sample record showed title, sample address, saved year, link result, note, and Keep/Review later/Archive controls. The full demo contained six source records spanning alive, dead, restricted, moved or changed, duplicate, and failed results.
- Editing a note and decision created only `localStorage["demo:bookmark-freshness-review:v1"]`. Pre-seeded real archive and license sentinels did not change.
- **Reset demo** removed the demo key and restored the original note. **Download extension and exit demo** removed demo state, preserved both real sentinels, returned home, and downloaded `bookmark-freshness-review.zip`.
- The full cold-home and demo edit/reset/exit request log was same-origin only. No analytics, advertising, font CDN, bookmark-site, or Sociobot request occurred.
- A live `/?demo=1&license=…` probe stripped the license parameter, made no license request, and stored no real or demo license.
- An aborted live license verification showed that the 50-check limit still applied, displayed no paid state, and stored no token.
